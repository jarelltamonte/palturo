Agriculture & Livelihood Skills Exchange

Practical food-growing, fishing, and local trade skills shared by people who work with land and water.

Find a mentor in Agriculture & Livelihood All categories

Agriculture and livelihood skills are best learned where they are used: in a garden, on a boat, at a market stall, or beside someone who has spent years doing the work. A skills exchange lets you learn those practical foundations from a community member instead of a generic course.

PalTuro connects learners with mentors who can teach farming, fishing, and local trades through conversation and hands-on practice. You bring curiosity and a skill to share; your mentor brings experience from their own livelihood.

Who this is for

This category is for anyone who wants to learn these skills from a person who practices them. You might be a beginner, a returning learner, or someone who wants to refresh a skill for work or family life.

You do not need to be an expert to join. If you can offer a different skill in return, you have something to bring to the exchange.

How a skills exchange works

A skills exchange is a reciprocal arrangement: you learn something you want, and you offer something you can teach in return. It is not a paid course and it is not a one-way lecture. The exchange is built around two people who each have something useful to share.

On PalTuro, you create a profile with the skills you can teach and the skills you want to learn. Matching looks at both directions, so the mentor you find is also getting a learner who can offer something back. Chat opens only after both people accept a request.

Why learn these skills through a person, not a feed

Learning these skills is easier to understand when someone can watch what you are doing, correct the small mistakes, and explain why a step matters. A mentor can adapt the pace to your materials, your space, and your experience.

That feedback loop is the advantage of a community skills exchange. You are not collecting videos; you are building a relationship with someone who can answer the question you did not know how to ask.

Agriculture & Livelihood skills exchange questions

What Agriculture & Livelihood skills can I learn?

The Agriculture & Livelihood category includes Farming, Fishing, Local Trades. Each skill page explains what you can learn, what to prepare, and how to find a mentor.

Is the Agriculture & Livelihood skills exchange free?

Yes. PalTuro is free to join. The exchange is reciprocal: you learn a skill and offer something you can teach in return.

What agriculture skills can I learn through a skills exchange?

Common starting points include vegetable gardening, soil preparation, planting schedules, fishing basics, and small local trades. The exact skills depend on the mentors in your community.

Do I need land or equipment to start?

Not necessarily. Many learners start with container gardening, a shared plot, or observation sessions. Ask your mentor what you need before the first meeting.

Is a skills exchange free?

PalTuro is free to join. The exchange is reciprocal: you teach something you know and learn something you want to learn.
